Why this matters to government

Inflation & CPI

Airfare sits inside CPI. If it spikes and nobody sees it early, the official story arrives late.

Household costs

Dearer flights hit families, migrant workers, and small businesses that depend on air links.

Tax & fiscal pressure

Ticket taxes and aviation revenue move with fares. Policy teams need a clean daily signal, not guesswork.

Fuel & shocks

Oil, festivals, and corridor disruptions show up in fares first. Daily measurement catches that.
