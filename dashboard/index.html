AirPriceX

A daily airfare inflation signal for government — not a booking tip.

Official CPI airfare is slow and hand-collected. We measure the same 8 routes with the same rules every day, turn them into one number, and keep a receipt trail MoSPI-style users can audit. Travellers already know “book early.” That is not what we sell.

Quick answers

Why not more routes?

Inflation needs a fixed basket. Expanding routes later = separate market watch, not a new CPI number mid-stream.

Why show booking windows?

Only as a check that the sample is healthy. The headline is the daily index — not “when to buy a ticket.”

Live scrapers?

Own collectors. Captcha → stop (no bypass) → sample fallback. Honesty over fake completeness.
