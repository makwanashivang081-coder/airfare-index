What this is

AirPriceX publishes two series from the same trusted observation store. The scraper never computes an index. The index never talks to Playwright. The desk never queries scraper internals.

  • Fixed domestic basket, high-traffic corridors, published weights.
  • Trust path: raw → normalise → identity → quality.
  • Missing is missing. It is never imputed as ₹0.
  • OTAs can inform the real-time market. They cannot enter the CPI sample.
